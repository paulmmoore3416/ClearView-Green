## Terms & Conditions

**1. Scope of Work**  
ClearView Green agrees to provide the listed services at the agreed frequency. Additional services not listed above will be quoted and approved separately before being performed.

**2. Invoicing & Payment Terms**  
Invoices will be issued via email on the day of each completed service visit.  
Payment terms: **Net-7** (due within 7 business days of invoice date).  
Accepted payment methods: Business check, ACH/bank transfer, credit/debit card via Square, Venmo Business, Zelle.

**3. Late Payment**  
Invoices not paid within 14 days of due date will be subject to a 1.5% monthly late fee on the outstanding balance. ClearView Green reserves the right to suspend service until past-due balances are resolved.

**4. Contract Duration & Cancellation**  
Month-to-month contracts may be cancelled by either party with **14 days' written notice**.  
Multi-month contracts require **30 days' written notice** for cancellation.  
Early termination by the Client of a multi-month agreement will result in a one-time cancellation fee equal to one month's contract value.

**5. Rate Adjustments**  
ClearView Green will provide a minimum of **30 days' written notice** of any rate changes. Rate changes will not apply to in-progress contract periods.

**6. Weather & Scheduling**  
ClearView Green reserves the right to reschedule any visit due to unsafe weather conditions. Rescheduled visits will occur within 3 business days at no additional charge.

**7. Access**  
Client agrees to provide reliable access to all service areas at the scheduled time. If ClearView Green arrives and access is unavailable, a $25 access fee may be charged for that visit.

**8. Pre-Existing Conditions**  
ClearView Green is not responsible for pre-existing glass damage, failed seals, damaged frames, or deteriorated caulking. Pre-existing conditions found during service will be photographed and documented.

**9. Liability**  
ClearView Green carries general liability insurance. A certificate of insurance (COI) is available upon request. In the event of damage caused by ClearView Green, the matter will be addressed promptly through our insurance carrier.

**10. Organic Solution**  
All services are performed using ClearView Green's 100% organic, plant-derived cleaning solution. No ammonia, phosphates, bleach, or synthetic solvents are used. SDS sheets for all ingredients are available upon request.

**11. Key & Access Code Management**  
If Client provides key(s) or access codes to ClearView Green, this information will be stored securely and used solely for the purpose of scheduled service access. ClearView Green will not share access information with any third party.

**12. Confidentiality**  
Any access information, business layout, or operational details observed during service visits will be treated as confidential and not shared with third parties.
