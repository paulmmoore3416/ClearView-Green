## Terms & Conditions

**1. Scope of Work**  
ClearView Green agrees to perform only the services listed above. Any additional services requested on the day of service will be quoted and approved before proceeding.

**2. Pricing**  
The agreed price above is a flat-rate quote for the described services. No additional charges will be assessed without prior customer approval.

**3. Payment**  
Payment is due upon completion of services. ClearView Green accepts cash, credit/debit card (via Square), Venmo, and Zelle.

**4. Cancellation Policy**  
Cancellations made with 24 or more hours' notice: no charge.  
Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice: a $25 late cancellation fee may apply.  
ClearView Green reserves the right to reschedule services due to weather conditions that would compromise safety or quality of results. No cancellation fee applies to weather-related rescheduling.

**5. Satisfaction Guarantee**  
If the Customer is not satisfied with the results of any service, ClearView Green will return within 48 hours to address any concerns at no additional charge, provided the concern is reported within 24 hours of service completion.

**6. Pre-Existing Conditions**  
ClearView Green is not responsible for pre-existing damage including but not limited to: cracked glass, fogged double-pane seals, damaged screens, deteriorated caulk or weatherstripping, or paint damage prior to service. The technician will photograph and document any pre-existing conditions noted during service.

**7. Property Access**  
The Customer grants ClearView Green permission to access the exterior of the property, including the use of ladders on the grounds, for the purpose of completing the services described above. For interior services, access is limited to rooms containing the windows to be cleaned.

**8. Liability**  
ClearView Green carries general liability insurance. In the event of damage caused by ClearView Green during service, the matter will be addressed through our insurance carrier. ClearView Green is not responsible for water damage resulting from windows left open, pre-existing failed window seals, or non-sealed building penetrations.

ClearView Green uses a 100% organic, plant-derived cleaning solution that is non-toxic, biodegradable, and safe for use around people, pets, and landscaping. SDS sheets for all solution ingredients are available upon request.
