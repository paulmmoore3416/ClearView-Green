## Purpose

To provide a clear, professional framework for collecting past-due amounts on commercial accounts while preserving business relationships wherever possible.

## Scope

Applies to commercial accounts on Net-7 payment terms. Residential accounts are expected to pay at time of service completion.

---

## Collections Timeline

| Days Past Due | Action |
|---|---|
| Day 1â€“7 | Invoice sent same day as service. No action. |
| Day 8 (1 day past due) | Courtesy reminder email/text: *"Just a reminder, invoice #[X] for $[amount] was due [date]."* |
| Day 14 (7 days past due) | Second notice via email and phone call. Offer to resolve payment method issues. |

## Write-Off Policy

Accounts with balances under $50 that have been in collections for 90+ days with no response may be written off as a business loss after the formal notice period has passed.
