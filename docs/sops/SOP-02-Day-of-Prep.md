# SOP-02: Day-of-Job Preparation

**Business:** ClearView Green  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2024  
**Owner:** Operations / Field

---

## Purpose

To ensure the technician arrives on every job site fully prepared, on time, professionally presented, and with all required equipment and solutions loaded and ready.

---

## Scope

Applies to every scheduled service day, for both residential and commercial jobs.

---

## Night-Before Checklist (Evening Prior to Job Day)

Complete the following the evening before each scheduled work day:

### Solution Preparation
- [ ] Mix fresh ClearView Green formula for the following day's jobs (see: [SOP-08: Formula Mixing](SOP-08-Formula-Mixing.md))
- [ ] Fill spray bottles (3x 32 oz minimum)
- [ ] Fill bucket with pre-mixed solution and seal with lid for transport
- [ ] Verify distilled water supply is adequate (minimum 2 gallons on hand)

### Equipment Check
- [ ] Squeegees inspected â€” rubber channels clean, no nicks or cracks. Replace rubber if nicked.
- [ ] T-bar applicator/washer sleeve â€” check for tears. Sleeve should be clean and free of grit.
- [ ] Extension pole â€” locking mechanism functions properly
- [ ] Scraper blades â€” fresh blade loaded, spare blades in kit
- [ ] Microfiber cloths â€” washed, clean, folded (minimum 12 fresh cloths per job day)
- [ ] Ladders inspected â€” feet in good condition, no bent rungs, locking mechanisms functional
- [ ] Vehicle loaded: ladders secured on roof/rack, bucket, spray bottles, poles, cloths, scraper, apron, knee pads

### Administrative Check
- [ ] Job log reviewed â€” confirm next day's jobs, addresses, and arrival times
- [ ] Reminder texts sent to all customers scheduled for tomorrow
- [ ] Phone charged overnight
- [ ] Invoice/receipt app ready (Square or equivalent)

## Arrival Protocol

1. **Arrive 2â€“5 minutes early.** Never more than 10 minutes late without a call/text en route.
2. **Park** without blocking the driveway, mailbox, or neighbor's access.
3. **First impression:** Branded shirt on. No eating/smoking/vaping in front of or near customer property.
4. **Greet the customer professionally.** Introduce yourself by name.
5. **Walk-around confirmation:** Walk the perimeter with the customer (or describe verbally) and confirm scope before starting:

   > *"Good morning, [Name]. I'm [Your Name] from ClearView Green. Today I'll be [description of service] for $[amount] â€” does that match what you were expecting? Any areas you'd especially like me to focus on?"*

6. **Get verbal confirmation** before touching any glass or setting up equipment.

## Equipment Staging

- Place bucket, spray bottles, and tool bag at job starting point (typically front of house, ground level)
- Do NOT lean ladders against gutters without ladder standoffs installed
- Do NOT prop poles or squeegees against customer vehicles or landscaping
- Keep walkways clear of equipment at all times â€” trip hazard liability
