# SOP-05: Commercial Window Cleaning

**Business:** ClearView Green  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2024  
**Owner:** Field Operations

---

## Purpose

To deliver consistent, professional, and minimally disruptive window cleaning services to commercial clients including storefronts, office buildings, restaurants, and retail locations.

---

## Scope

Applies to all commercial window cleaning contracts and one-time commercial jobs.

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

### Phase 1: Arrival & Setup

1. Arrive at scheduled time. Greet the business contact professionally.
2. Confirm scope with contact before starting: *"Today I'll be cleaning [description] â€” does that match your expectation?"*
3. For public-facing storefronts: place **"Work in Progress"** signage at both ends of the work zone to alert pedestrians before beginning.
4. Do not block entrance doors during the workday. Start with windows furthest from main entrance.

---

### Phase 2: Storefront Exterior Glass

**Standard Storefront Sequence:**

1. **Pre-rinse** â€” spray the entire glass face with solution from T-bar or spray bottle.
2. **Scrub** â€” apply T-bar in horizontal strokes, full width, top to bottom.
3. **Squeegee** â€” single long horizontal pull for wide panes. Start at top, overlap each pass by 1 inch. Wipe rubber after every pull.
4. **Edge detail** â€” tight microfiber fold on all edges. Pay special attention to where frame meets glass â€” grime accumulates in this gap and is highly visible from outside.
5. **Kick-plate glass / transom windows** â€” clean separately using smaller squeegee.
6. **Glass doors (exterior)** â€” clean both door faces and door frame glass inserts. Wipe door handle escutcheon areas.

---

### Phase 3: Commercial Interior (When Applicable)

Follow [SOP-04: Interior Window Clean](SOP-04-Interior-Clean.md) with the following commercial additions:

- Notify staff in each area before beginning work in that area.
- Do not move commercial display merchandise â€” ask staff to clear the window area.
- For restaurant interiors: do not spray solution near open food prep areas. Coordinate with manager.
- Keep squeegee pulls controlled â€” commercial panes are often larger and carry more water.

### Phase 5: Invoice & Payment

- Provide invoice via email **same day** as service.
- Payment terms for commercial accounts: **Net-7** (due within 7 days of invoice date).
- Accepted payment: card via Square, ACH/bank transfer, business check, Venmo Business, Zelle.
- For recurring contracts: send invoice on the same day each billing cycle.
- For accounts 14+ days past due: call and follow up with a written notice. See [Collections Policy](../business-docs/Collections-Policy.md).

## Recurring Commercial Contract Management

For clients on a recurring schedule:

| Task | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Confirm next scheduled date | After every completed visit |
| Review scope for changes | Quarterly |
| Rate adjustment notice | 30-day written notice required |
| Contract renewal review | Annually |

Keep a commercial client folder for each account containing:
- Signed service agreement
- Invoice history
- Contact list (primary + backup)
- Access instructions (door codes, key location, etc.)
- Special notes (no-go zones, preferred solution, etc.)
