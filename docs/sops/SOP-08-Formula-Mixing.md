## Standard Formula â€” Working Solution (Per Gallon)

This is the formula used for 95% of all jobs.

| Ingredient | Amount per Gallon | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Distilled water | 128 oz (fill to top after adding others) | Base carrier |
| White distilled vinegar (5%) | 2 oz (4 tablespoons) | Degreaser, mineral solvent |
| Castile soap, unscented | 1â€“2 drops ONLY | Surfactant (more = residue) |
| Isopropyl alcohol 70% | 1 oz (2 tablespoons) | Anti-streak, fast-dry agent |

> **Warning:** Do NOT use more than 2 drops of castile soap per gallon. Excess soap creates suds that leave a film on glass, requiring a second pass. When in doubt â€” use 1 drop.

### Mixing Steps

1. Start with a clean 1-gallon mixing jug or bucket (no soap residue).
2. Add distilled water first â€” fill to approximately 120 oz.
3. Add vinegar. Stir gently with a clean stir stick. Do not shake (creates foam).
4. Add **1 drop** castile soap. Stir gently.
5. Add isopropyl alcohol. Stir gently.
6. Top off with distilled water to the 128 oz mark.
7. Cap and label:

```
ClearView Green Working Solution
Batch Date: ________
Standard Formula
```

## Hard Water / Mineral Stain Treatment (Separate Formula)

For heavy calcium, lime, or silicate deposits on glass.

**Ingredients:**
- Undiluted white distilled vinegar
- Baking soda (sodium bicarbonate)
- 0000 (four-aught) steel wool â€” glass-safe grade only

**Procedure:**
1. Apply undiluted vinegar to stained area with a soaked microfiber cloth. Let sit 5 minutes.
2. If stains persist: mix a paste of vinegar + baking soda (approximately 2:1 ratio). Apply with microfiber and scrub with light pressure.
3. For severely etched glass: use 0000 steel wool on **wet glass only** in small circular motions. Test a small inconspicuous area first.
4. Rinse thoroughly with distilled water.
5. Apply standard formula and squeegee to finish.

> **Note:** Severe mineral etching (frosted/hazy appearance even when wet) cannot be removed by cleaning â€” it is physical damage to the glass. Notify the customer and document with a photo.

## Post-Construction Clean Formula

For paint overspray, silicone, or adhesive residue on glass.

**Ingredients:**
- ClearView Green standard formula
- Scraper (with new blade)
- Isopropyl alcohol (full strength, applied directly with cloth for adhesive)

**Procedure:**
1. Wet the glass thoroughly with standard formula.
2. Hold scraper at 30Â°. Scrape paint specks in one-directional strokes. **Never scrape dry glass.**
3. For silicone/caulk residue: apply 70% isopropyl directly with cloth. Let sit 60 seconds. Scrape carefully.
4. Rinse and squeegee to finish with standard formula.

## Storage & Disposal

- Store labeled jugs in a cool, dry location away from direct sunlight.

- Spent/used solution: dispose via standard drain â€” biodegrades completely. No special disposal required.
