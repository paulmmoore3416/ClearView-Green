## Purpose

To safely deliver pressure washing services on driveways, sidewalks, patios, decks, and home exteriors using appropriate equipment settings and, where possible, ClearView Green's organic pre-treatment solution.

---

## Scope

Applies to all pressure washing jobs including driveways, sidewalks, patios, decks, concrete, brick, vinyl siding, and painted surfaces.

## Organic Pre-Treatment Application

Where possible, apply ClearView Green pre-treatment before pressure washing:

**Pre-Treatment Mix (for pressure washer soap tank):**
- 1 part white vinegar
- 1 part water
- 2â€“4 drops castile soap per gallon

Apply to surface using low-pressure soap setting. Allow 3â€“5 minutes dwell time. Then rinse with high pressure. This lifts biological growth (mildew, algae) more effectively and reduces the pressure needed to clean, protecting the surface.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

### Phase 1: Preparation

1. Walk the job area and:
   - Identify surface types and select appropriate PSI (see table above).
   - Note pre-existing cracks, loose mortar, flaking paint â€” photograph and notify customer.
   - Locate and cover (or turn off) exterior outlets, light fixtures, and HVAC intakes in the work zone.
   - Move potted plants, furniture, and vehicles out of the spray zone.
   - Close all windows and doors within 10 feet of work zone.
2. Wet test a small inconspicuous area at your intended PSI before proceeding. Look for surface damage or paint lifting.
3. Place "Work In Progress" signage at any entry points to the work area.

---

### Phase 2: Pressure Washing

1. Apply organic pre-treatment with low-pressure soap nozzle. Allow dwell time.
2. Start at the farthest point from the drain/lowest point. Work toward drainage.
3. Hold wand at consistent height (6â€“12 inches from surface for concrete; 12â€“18 inches for soft surfaces).
4. Use overlapping passes â€” no more than 50% pass overlap.
5. Keep wand moving at all times â€” holding in one spot damages surfaces.
6. Work in sections. Rinse sections completely before moving to the next.
7. Flush all debris toward appropriate drainage points â€” street curb, yard edge, or designated drain.

---

### Phase 3: Rinse & Inspection

1. Final full-surface rinse with clean water at 40Â° nozzle.
2. Inspect for missed areas, remaining staining, or surface damage.
3. Allow 15-minute standing time and visually inspect as surface dries.
4. Photograph the finished area.

---

### Phase 4: Closeout

1. Remove and store all signage and equipment.
2. Return customer's furniture, plants, and other items to original positions (if moved).
3. Confirm exterior outlets are uncovered and functioning.
4. Customer walk-through and payment per SOP-06.

## Safety Requirements

- **Mandatory PPE:** Safety glasses, closed-toe shoes, hearing protection (if using gas-powered unit), gloves.
- Do not operate pressure washer on a ladder â€” trip and fall risk from recoil force.
- Never point wand at people, animals, or vehicles.
- Minimum 10-foot clearance from overhead power lines at all times.
- Chemical burns: even organic solutions applied under high pressure can cause skin irritation. Rinse any skin contact with water.

---

## Surface Damage Policy

If pressure washing reveals underlying damage (rotted wood, failing caulk, cracked concrete) that was not apparent during the initial walk-through:

1. Stop work on the affected section.
2. Photograph and notify the customer.
3. Document in job notes.
4. Do not attempt repairs outside the scope of window/cleaning services.
