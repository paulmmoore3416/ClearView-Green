# SOP-04: Residential Interior Window Cleaning

**Business:** ClearView Green  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2024  
**Owner:** Field Operations

---

## Purpose

To deliver a streak-free, damage-free interior window cleaning service while protecting customer property and maintaining professional conduct inside the home.

---

## Scope

Applies to all interior window cleaning work on residential properties.

---

## Required Equipment

- [ ] ClearView Green formula in spray bottles (NOT bucket pour â€” reduces drip risk indoors)
- [ ] Squeegees: 6", 10", 14" (smaller sizes preferred for indoor panes)

- [ ] Scraper with fresh blade (for adhesive residue, stickers, paint)

## Pre-Entry Protocol

Before entering any customer home:

1. Put on shoe covers before crossing the threshold. Carry extras.
2. Ask the customer: *"Is there anything fragile or any areas near windows you'd like me to avoid?"*
3. Do a quick visual sweep for:
   - Valuables on windowsills or near windows
   - Pets that need to be secured (our organic formula is pet-safe, but animals near ladders/equipment are a hazard)
   - Window treatments (curtains, blinds, shutters) that need to be moved â€” ask the customer to move them, or request permission to do so carefully
4. Never move furniture without explicit customer permission.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

### For Each Window:

**Step 1 â€” Lay Drop Protection**  
Place a folded microfiber towel or drop cloth on the sill and floor beneath the window before spraying anything.

**Step 2 â€” Spray Application**  
Use spray bottle only. Spray a light-to-medium mist directly onto the glass â€” avoid over-saturating. Excess solution drips onto walls, trim, and floors.  
For heavy grime: spray, wait 20 seconds, then scrub before squeegeeing.

**Step 3 â€” Scrub**  
Use a damp microfiber cloth or small T-bar applicator. Scrub the entire pane including corners. For stickers or tape residue: use scraper at 30Â° on wet glass only â€” single-direction strokes.

**Step 4 â€” Squeegee**  
- Prefer **straight-pull method** for interior (less water displacement than fan method).
- Start at the top left corner. Pull straight across or straight down in overlapping strokes.
- Wipe rubber with dry cloth after every pull.
- Keep squeegee blade tight to glass â€” do not lift mid-pull.

**Step 5 â€” Edge Detail**  
Fold a dry microfiber cloth into a firm pad. Wipe all four edges. Pay extra attention to bottom edge â€” drips onto sill or floor are the #1 customer complaint on interior jobs.

**Step 6 â€” Sill & Frame Wipe**  
Wipe sill and lower frame dry. Pick up drop cloth. If solution dripped onto floor or wall: wipe immediately with dry cloth. Do not let solution dry on painted surfaces.

**Step 7 â€” Replace Window Treatments**  
Return any curtains, blinds, or shutters to their original position. Straighten anything disturbed.

Before leaving each room:

## Handling Customer Property Near Windows

- Photograph the window area before moving anything, if valuables are present.
- Gently move small items to a clear surface nearby â€” never to the floor.
- Replace items exactly as found after cleaning.
- If a customer item is accidentally damaged: stop work, notify the customer immediately, take a photo, document in job log. Do not attempt to hide, fix, or minimize the damage. Contact your liability insurance provider.

---

## Multi-Story Interior (Atrium Windows, Stairwell Windows)

- Use extension pole from safe ground position where possible.
- If ladder required inside the home: use rubber-footed step ladder only. Place rubber non-slip mat under feet.
- Never lean ladder against walls or painted surfaces â€” use wall guards on ladder tops.
- Confirm with customer that ladder use inside is approved before setup.
