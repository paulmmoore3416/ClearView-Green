# SOP-09: Gutter Cleaning

**Business:** ClearView Green  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2024  
**Owner:** Field Operations

## Purpose

To safely and thoroughly clean gutters and downspouts on residential and commercial properties, preventing water damage and extending roof system life.

---

- [ ] Extension ladder (rated Type IA, 300 lb)
- [ ] Ladder standoffs / V-stands

## Step-by-Step Procedure

### Phase 1: Assessment

1. Walk the perimeter of the property and note:
   - Gutter condition: sagging sections, separated joints, visible holes
   - Downspout count and locations
   - Trees directly overhead (indicates heavy debris likely)
   - Any visible obstructions (bird nests, large debris piles)
2. Photograph any pre-existing gutter damage before beginning work.
3. Notify customer of any visible damage found â€” document with photo before touching.

---

### Phase 2: Debris Removal

1. Position ladder at one end of a gutter run (typically a corner or downspout end).
2. Hang bucket from ladder rung with an S-hook.
3. Using the gutter scoop, remove all debris (leaves, twigs, granules, mud) and deposit into bucket.
4. Move ladder every 3â€“4 feet along the gutter run â€” never overreach.
5. Deposit bucket contents into a yard waste bag at ground level.
6. Repeat for all gutter sections on all four sides.

> Never lean to the side beyond the ladder stiles. Descend and reposition.

---

### Phase 3: Flush & Flow Test

1. After all debris is removed, use a garden hose (or low-PSI pressure washer) to flush each gutter section toward the nearest downspout.
2. Confirm water flows freely to all downspouts â€” no standing water or overflow.
3. At each downspout: flush from top and confirm water exits freely at the bottom.

**Clogged Downspout Procedure:**
1. Insert hose into downspout opening from the top. Apply full pressure.
2. If still clogged: use a flexible plumber's snake or high-pressure downspout nozzle.
3. If blockage cannot be cleared without disassembly: notify customer â€” additional downspout service required (quote separately).

---

### Phase 4: Cleanup

1. Remove all debris bags from property. Do not leave debris in customer's yard.
2. Rinse any debris that fell on walkways or landscaping.
3. Pick up drop cloth.
4. Wipe ladder feet before loading to prevent gutter grit from scratching vehicle or surfaces on next job.

---

### Phase 5: Closeout

1. Walk customer through the work performed (if present).
2. Show photos of any damage found.
3. Collect payment per SOP-06.
4. Recommend window cleaning bundle if not already booked (natural upsell â€” they're already home).

---

## Safety Reminders Specific to Gutter Work

- Gutter debris often contains mold, bird and rodent feces, and sharp metal debris. **Gloves and safety glasses are mandatory.**
- Wet leaves make gutters heavy â€” secure bucket to ladder before filling.
- Do not lean ladder against gutters that appear loose or detached from fascia.
- In fall/winter: beware of ice inside gutter channels. Do not probe frozen gutters with metal tools.
