## Hierarchy of Controls

ClearView Green applies the standard EH&S hierarchy of hazard controls to all field operations:

1. **Elimination** â€” Remove the hazard entirely (e.g., use water-fed pole to avoid ladder use)
2. **Substitution** â€” Replace with a safer alternative (e.g., organic formula instead of caustic cleaners)
3. **Engineering Controls** â€” Physical safeguards (ladder standoffs, stabilizers)
4. **Administrative Controls** â€” SOPs, checklists, weather decision matrices
5. **PPE** â€” Last line of defense (gloves, glasses, knee pads)

## Ladder Safety

### Selection
| Height Required | Equipment |
|---|---|
| Up to 10 ft | 6-ft fiberglass step ladder |
| 10â€“20 ft | Extension ladder (rated 300 lbs Type IA) |
| Above 20 ft | Do not service without fall arrest system |
| Soft/uneven ground | Use ladder leveler feet |

### Set-Up Rules
- **4-to-1 Rule:** For every 4 feet of height, the base must be 1 foot out from the wall.
- Always use **ladder standoffs (V-stands)** when leaning near gutters â€” prevents gutter damage and provides a wider, more stable stance.
- Inspect ladder before use: no bent rungs, feet in good condition, locking mechanism positive-locked.
- Never set a ladder on wet grass without feet firmly planted; use rubber foot pads on hard surfaces.
- Never set a ladder in front of an unlocked door â€” lock the door or post a barrier.

### Use Rules
- **Three points of contact** at all times when ascending/descending.
- Never stand on the top 2 rungs of a step ladder or the top 3 rungs of an extension ladder.
- No leaning sideways beyond hip-width of the ladder stiles â€” descend and reposition.
- Tools in apron â€” do not carry tools while climbing.
- Call out **"Coming down"** before descending in areas where others may be present.
- Never leave a raised ladder unattended â€” lay it flat or secure it.

## Chemical Safety (ClearView Green Organic Formula)

ClearView Green's formula is composed of food-grade, GRAS (Generally Recognized As Safe) ingredients. However, safe handling practices apply:

| Hazard | Precaution |
|---|---|
| Eye contact (vinegar or isopropyl) | Rinse with clean water for 15 minutes. Seek medical attention if irritation persists. |
| Skin contact | Rinse with water. No harm expected from formula ingredients at working dilution. |
| Ingestion | Not a concern at working dilution, but do not intentionally ingest. |
| Spill | Wipe with cloth. Solution is biodegradable â€” no special disposal required. |
| Storage | Store in labeled containers in cool, dry location. Keep away from open flame (isopropyl is flammable in concentrate). |

**SDS Sheets** for all formula ingredients are maintained and available upon request. As an EH&S-managed business, complete SDS documentation is on file for:
- White distilled vinegar (acetic acid solution)
- Castile soap (potassium olivate / coconut oil soap)
- Isopropyl alcohol 70%
- Distilled water

## Electrical Safety

- Do not operate water-fed poles or spray equipment near overhead power lines.
- Minimum clearance from power lines: **10 feet horizontal, 10 feet vertical** for any wet equipment.
- Do not clean utility room windows, breaker box areas, or exterior HVAC equipment.
- Use **fiberglass (non-conductive) ladders only** â€” never aluminum ladders near electrical hazards.

## Pressure Washing Safety (When Applicable)

- Never direct pressure washer nozzle toward people, animals, or vehicles.
- Do not pressure wash within 3 feet of open windows, vents, or exterior outlets.
- Use appropriate PSI for surface: 1200â€“1500 PSI for wood/vinyl; up to 3000 PSI for concrete.
- Always wear safety glasses and closed-toe shoes during pressure washing.
- Do not pressure wash on a ladder â€” trip and fall hazard from recoil.

## Property Protection

- Photograph condition of any pre-existing damage (cracked glass, damaged screens, chipped frames) **before** beginning work. Send photo to customer via text.
- Do not attempt to clean cracked glass â€” stop, notify customer, document.
- Do not rest ladders against painted surfaces without foam/rubber protection on ladder ends.
- Lay drop cloths under all work areas on decks, patios, and painted surfaces.

## Incident Reporting

In the event of any injury or property damage:

**Immediately:**
1. Ensure safety of all parties involved.
2. Call 911 if medical emergency.
3. Photograph the incident scene.
4. Notify the customer promptly and honestly.

**Within 24 Hours:**
1. Complete the [Incident Report Form](../business-docs/Incident-Report-Form.md).
2. Contact ClearView Green's general liability insurance provider.
3. Do not make any promises of payment or admission of fault before consulting insurer.
