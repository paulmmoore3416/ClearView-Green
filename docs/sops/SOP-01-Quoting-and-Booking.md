# SOP-01: Quoting & Customer Booking

**Business:** ClearView Green  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2024  
**Owner:** Operations

---

## Purpose

To ensure every prospective customer receives a prompt, accurate, flat-rate quote and a smooth booking experience that sets expectations before the technician arrives on site.

---

## Scope

Applies to all inbound inquiries received via phone, text, email, social media, or online scheduling forms.

---

| Channel | Target Response Time |

| Nextdoor / Facebook DM | Respond within 1 hour |
