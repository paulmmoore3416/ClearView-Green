## Purpose

## Scope

Applies to all residential exterior window cleaning services, including single-story, two-story, and mixed-height properties.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

### Phase 1: Assessment & Setup

1. Walk the entire exterior perimeter before starting. Note:
   - Cracked, fogged, broken-seal, or damaged panes (photograph and notify customer â€” do not clean cracked glass)
   - Screen condition â€” note any damaged/missing screens before touching
   - Paint overspray, heavy oxidation, or hard water staining (may require separate treatment â€” quote accordingly)
   - Obstacles: landscaping, furniture, AC units near windows

2. Set up equipment at the starting point (front of house, left side). Work clockwise around the house, left to right, top to bottom.

3. Confirm ladder placement before ascending any elevated surface.

---

### Phase 2: Ground-Level Windows

For each window pane:

**Step 1 â€” Pre-clean**  
Remove large debris (cobwebs, leaves, insect nests) with a dry brush or dry cloth before applying solution.

**Step 2 â€” Wet**  
Dip T-bar in bucket (or spray with spray bottle for smaller panes). Apply solution to the entire pane including all four corners. Scrub with moderate pressure using the washer sleeve.

**Step 3 â€” Scrape (if needed)**  
For paint specks, bug splatter, or adhesive residue: hold scraper at a 30Â° angle, wet glass only, and scrape in one-directional strokes. Never scrape dry glass.

**Step 4 â€” Squeegee**  
Select squeegee width closest to (but not wider than) the pane.

- *For large panes (18"+):* Fan technique â€” start at top corner, arc across in overlapping S-pulls.
- *For small/medium panes:* Straight-pull method â€” top to bottom, overlapping 1 inch.
- Wipe the squeegee rubber with a dry microfiber cloth after **every single pull**.

**Step 5 â€” Detail Edges**  
Fold a dry microfiber cloth into a tight pad. Wipe all four edges of the pane. Pay special attention to bottom edge where water pools. Wipe sill dry â€” water left on wood sills causes rot.

**Step 6 â€” Quality Check**  
Step back 3â€“5 feet. Inspect the pane from three angles:
- Straight on
- 45Â° from left
- 45Â° from right (backlighting reveals streaks)

If any streaks detected: re-spray lightly, re-squeegee that section, re-detail edge.

### Phase 3: Upper-Story Windows (Ladder or Pole)

**Extension Pole Method (preferred for up to 2 stories):**
1. Attach T-bar to pole. Dip in bucket or apply solution from a trigger bottle attached to pole.
2. Scrub pane from left edge to right edge, top to bottom.
3. Attach squeegee to pole. Straight pulls, overlapping, top to bottom. Wipe rubber between each pull using a cloth clipped to your apron.
4. Detail edges with a pole-mounted detail cloth.

**Ladder Method (when pole cannot reach or for complex frames):**
1. Position ladder per safety guidelines in [SOP-07](SOP-07-Safety.md).
2. Never stand on the top 2 rungs of a step ladder or the top 3 rungs of an extension ladder.
3. All tools in apron holster â€” never hold tools in hand while ascending or descending.
4. Call out "Coming down" before descending.

---

### Phase 4: Screen Cleaning (Add-On)

1. Photograph each screen in position before removal (helps with reinstallation).
2. Remove screens carefully. Note any screens that are torn, bent, or missing hardware â€” notify customer and photograph.
3. Lay screens flat on a clean drop cloth.
4. Scrub both sides of each screen with wet T-bar applicator + ClearView Green solution.
5. Rinse with clean water (garden hose or bucket).
6. Shake excess water off. Pat with microfiber. **Do not let screens air-dry in direct sunlight** â€” they water-spot.
7. Reinstall screens in correct windows using reference photos.

---

### Phase 5: Tracks & Sill Deep Clean (Add-On)

1. Vacuum or brush loose debris from track channels.
2. Apply solution to track. Use a detail brush or old toothbrush to scrub channels.
3. Wipe out with a damp cloth. Repeat until no residue remains.
4. Dry tracks completely â€” moisture in tracks can cause mold.

---

### Phase 6: Final Walkthrough

1. Walk the entire perimeter again.
2. Check every cleaned pane one final time.
3. Confirm all screens reinstalled in correct windows.
4. Confirm all sills, tracks, and surrounding trim are dry and clean.
5. Remove all equipment from customer property.
6. Proceed to [SOP-06: Post-Job Closeout](SOP-06-Post-Job-Closeout.md).

## Common Problems & Solutions

| Problem | Cause | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Streaks after squeegee | Dirty rubber, too much soap, direct sun | Wipe rubber more frequently; add less soap to mix; work in shade where possible |
| Water spots drying before squeegee | Hot/dry/sunny conditions | Work faster; add alcohol to mix; spray one pane at a time |
| Cloudy finish | Hard water residue | Use hard water treatment formula (see SOP-08) |
| Squeegee dragging | Nicked rubber | Replace rubber channel immediately |
| Screen won't reinstall | Frame bent during removal | Note for customer; do not force |
